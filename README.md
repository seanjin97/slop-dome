# Slop Iron Dome

[reference](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Iron_Dome)

## What it is

AI has it's own strengths and weaknesses. It's true that using AI can help to massively increase throughput to automate mundane tasks
such as scanning for cheap flights, mass sending out resumes, building and shipping apps.

AI, however, is not good for human communication. Nobody wants to read messages generated from AI. Lowkey disrespectful that we need to
read through AI generated content, spending time, effort, and sanity to read and filter through slopified text. That text which was probably
carelessly crafted by the sender.

We could go all blue pilled and reject the use of AI. We can try to educate and inform people that using AI to communicate is not the way to go. For example, existing literature such as [nohello.net](https://nohello.net/en/) let's people know not to drop just a "hi".

But what if we took the red pill instead? Let's just embrace the entirety of AI. If AI wants to speak to you, then let it, but not without routing through your personal
`Iron Dome` agent that will assess and duke it out with them before finally synthesisng the key outcomes to you in nice readable format, which you care about.

I am not against the use of AI. AI is a huge productivity tool that's good at abstracting away "behind the scene work" and helping us just focus on the outcomes.

What I'm saying is that communication is not an outcome. It is a medium to transmit information. Traditionally, we use our brains and voice to communicate ideas. In the modern world, AI communicates for us, until the point where it becomes significant enough that we should care about personally, then we take over the controls. Of course, we can say that why not let AI do the communication and eventually do the execution themselves? Yes, why not? It would lead to less miscomms maybe since AI has the same core brain and model of understanding each other. But until we feel (a human emotion) safe enough to do so, let AI talk to AI and then hand it off the important bits to us.

## Assumptions

1. Meat proxies are the mainstream
2. AI is great at AI to AI communication (think Opus orchestrating Chatgpt type shi)

## Implementation

1. If it looks and sounds human, then let the message through
2. Else, the `Iron Dome` will handle it and the 2 AIs can duke it out until a clear outcome agreement is achieved and both parties are notified on the key actionable outcome.
3. It must be clear that any AI message will be read by AI, and AI to AI communication is the default way of operating.
